FM RUSH v24.39 — SHOP PREVIEW

1. Extract the ZIP and run START_FM_RUSH.bat from Scanner Nov 20.
2. In Brave, go to chrome://extensions. Enable Developer mode, remove the older
   FM Rush extension entry, then Load unpacked from this ZIP's browser_extension
   folder. Refresh the HeneFM Deals page once.
3. Select MapleStory in the FM Rush strip. Use Refresh if you open or restart the
   game after FM Rush.
4. Click CHASE on a HeneFM deal. The yellow channel guide and the red shop
   preview appear immediately. The shop guide uses the map pin on that card.
5. Go to the indicated channel and FM room. The red box shows the shop's
   expected location from the entry camera. If the shop starts off-screen, the
   preview gives its direction. The guide is click-through.
6. Skip or unlock to clear the preview and choose a different listing.

Shop previews support 1280 × 720 and 1366 × 768 game clients. A deal needs a map
pin to show a preview. Camera movement can shift the game scene under the guide.
The guides use HeneFM map pins and fixed channel-menu positions. No game
screen capture or OCR is needed.

Ctrl+Alt+N skips. Tools > FM map opens the built-in portal map. Tools > Export
deal archive saves the listings grouped by IGN.
