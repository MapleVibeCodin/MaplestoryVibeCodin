FM RUSH v24.40 — SHOP PREVIEW

1. Extract the ZIP and run START_FM_RUSH.bat from Scanner Nov 20.
2. If you already use extension v24.39.0, keep it loaded. For a first install,
   go to chrome://extensions in Brave, enable Developer mode, and use Load
   unpacked to select browser_extension. Refresh the HeneFM Deals tab once.
3. Select MapleStory in the FM Rush strip. Use Refresh if you open or restart the
   game after FM Rush.
4. Click CHASE on a HeneFM deal. The yellow channel guide and the red shop
   preview appear immediately. The shop guide uses the map pin on that card.
5. Go to the indicated channel and FM room. The red box projects the shop
   from the room's entry portal. A red ring shows the shop in the target room's
   default minimap, even if the camera shifts. The guides are click-through.
6. If the camera spawned offset from the preview, point at the bottom center
   of the numbered doorway and press Ctrl+Alt+F. This realigns the large box
   for the current chase. If the shop is off-screen, the guide gives its direction.
7. Skip or unlock to clear the preview and choose a different listing.

Shop previews support 1280 × 720 and 1366 × 768 game clients. A deal needs a map
pin to show a preview. The large box may need realignment after you move.
The guides use HeneFM map pins and fixed channel-menu positions. No game
screen capture or OCR is needed.

Entry portal data: https://osmsdataexplorer.com/data/current/portals.json

Ctrl+Alt+N skips. Tools > FM map opens the built-in portal map. Tools > Export
deal archive saves the listings grouped by IGN.
