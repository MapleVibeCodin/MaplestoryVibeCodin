are archived/available but never assigned automatically. Newer explicit clicks
win over an older choice arriving late. SKIP ignores a card; skipping the current
choice or logging a purchase clears it without selecting another deal. Skipping
a different card preserves the current chase. Skipped cards can be chosen again.

outline and arrow, using a compact UI reference derived from the uploaded menu.

selected live seller is searched; three strong letters can produce CHECK NAME.

No automatic game clicks, purchases or forced next listing are introduced.
