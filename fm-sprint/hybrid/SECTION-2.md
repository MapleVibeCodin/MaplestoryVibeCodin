# Section 2 — validated visual bank
R3 bgr_array and visual_key extracted byte-for-byte from pinned aeb991d core. Added separately review-gated LRU; accepts only explicitly independently manually reviewed complete unobscured native labels, no blank, ellipsis, replacement/control characters or missing provenance, no OCR self-learning. Normalization is NFKC/casefold/whitespace. Qualified signatures are 20-byte fingerprints. Conflicts permanently quarantined within the bank instance, including conflicting requalification after positive LRU eviction, because bounded historical claims are separate. On history or quarantine capacity exhaustion the bank disables itself instead of forgetting old conflicts. Source/review metadata bounded. Lookup requires an explicit eligible row; current frame visibility/target policy must be enforced upstream. This is an in-memory component; labels not loaded from arbitrary OCR/caches.
