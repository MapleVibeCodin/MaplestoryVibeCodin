## Method

Same map/epoch/level/segment, fresh <=1s confident unestimated EXP contexts deduplicated by timestamp. Positive changes with gap <=3s decompose into exact base-EXP combinations using dynamic programming, independent species minima/maxima and total minima/maxima (not necessarily simultaneous). Unmatched and gap EXP remain unassigned. Matched EXP / endpoint net EXP is a model-coverage measure, not actual kill-count accuracy; <=80% effectively unqualified (code accepts 80% exactly); death, shared and bonus EXP can coincide with ordinary amounts. Use >=80% coverage and <=100% for conditional per-equivalent ratios, not actual personal kills. Elapsed group spans may include idle/uncertain intervals. V2.1 uses scoped accepted pickups; v2.3 uses sealed secondary reader alone; never sum the two ledgers. Unseen pickups are unknown, not zero. Ratios use the exact same group elapsed window for notification-derived receipts and reconstructed equivalents, not wallet endpoints. Wallet measured separately; drastic truncation rejects entire automatic wallet estimate. The reported known NPC subtotal includes only mapped observed species-linked Etc items and known unit prices, not other loot. No potion costs. Raw cash never assigned to individual species without evidence. All actual personal kill counts, raw mesos/mob and combined mesos/mob by species remain unknown. Prior level-weighted allocations are illustrative only, not used in this historical comparison. Missing-capture failure remains unresolved.
