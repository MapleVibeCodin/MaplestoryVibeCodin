FM RUSH v24.13 — ONE BOUGHT FOLDER

Close the old app, extract into a fresh folder, and run the top-level
START_FM_RUSH.bat. Confirm v24.13. No extension update is needed if you already
use extension 24.11.0; keep screenshot saving enabled.

After buying an item:
1. Select its listing in the FIND NAME queue.
2. Click BOUGHT · SAVE PNG / NEXT.
3. Click OPEN BOUGHT whenever you want to browse purchases for relisting.

All purchase screenshots go directly into Downloads/Bought, with no seller/date
subfolders. The original listing card retains its visible stats and resale
information. Filenames include item, seller and purchase price.

The app copies the exact listing PNG already saved by the extension, then marks
that deal complete and advances the queue. Original screenshots remain intact.
It never substitutes the newest card or a different listing from the same seller.
If the screenshot cannot be found, choose the FM_Rush_Item_Screenshots folder;
custom source folders are remembered. Cancel to wait for the original capture.
Missing/broken images or copy failures leave the deal pending.

Purchase status and screenshot path are kept in the persistent archive and its
JSON export. UNDO DONE reopens the deal but keeps the PNG. Clicking BOUGHT again
reuses that file. A failed archive write can be retried without duplicate PNGs.
Existing listings, archives, room-entry scanning and red markers are preserved.
