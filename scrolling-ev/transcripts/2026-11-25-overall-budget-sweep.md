If both your earring and overall are still clean, splitting 2m is much better:

| Allocation | Combined expected added stat | Added max range |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2m overall only | 10.21 | +13.53 |
| 1m overall + 1m earring | **15.07** | **+19.96** |

That is approximately 48% more added damage range for the same budget, assuming the previous independent earring model and fully convertible DEX.

**My spending plan: 750k initially on the overall, with permission to go up to 1m.** Spend the remainder on other clean or weak equipment before pushing the overall to 2m.

These are expected values, not guaranteed outcomes. Prices are carried forward, range uses illustrative 53 WATK, and the model assumes one active overall plus one parked fallback. The split comparison is not a joint equipment optimization.
