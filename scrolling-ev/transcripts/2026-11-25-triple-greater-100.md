## Simulation — 100 Pansy Earrings

I ran 100 independent 10% opening attempts, NPC'd every failed base, then applied two additional 10% scrolls to every successful earring, regardless of the second scroll's outcome.

Initial earrings: 100
Successful openers: 8
NPC'd earrings: 92

The chance of getting at least one +9 earring is only **9.52% per 100-base batch**. The chance of at least one +6 or better is 85.31%.

Our best two earrings are currently +6 with two slots remaining. Finishing either with two guaranteed Lesser scrolls costs another 69,560 and produces +8, or approximately +10.60 maximum range under our illustrative 53-attack model.

Expected net cost of repeating the entire 100-base strategy: 4,051,560 mesos.

This is one seeded random simulation, not actual in-game results. The expected counts and batch probabilities are calculated analytically.
