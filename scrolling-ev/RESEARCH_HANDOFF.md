## AP substitution — important correction
Earring DEX is worth exactly one LUK when it replaces one base DEX AP and the freed AP is assigned to LUK, holding total DEX constant. Applies across equipment slots. Example: required total DEX 50, other gear +10 DEX. +8 LUK earring with base DEX 40 and base LUK 222, or +8 DEX earring with base DEX 32 and base LUK 230, both give total DEX 50 and LUK 240 if other gear contributes +10 LUK. These AP totals were assumed in prior examples; verify actual level-50 AP rules before use.
For a fixed starting AP allocation and gear, let R be the reducible base DEX respecting the minimum legal base stat, all gear requirements, and equip order. For an earring adding d DEX and l LUK, choose x=min(d,R) if AP reallocation is allowed and affordable; total LUK increases l+x and total DEX increases d-x. Excess DEX has only its normal secondary-stat value. Model AP reset cost, availability and actual scope; do not assume free reset or that one reset changes all AP without fresh verification. If fixed AP, x=0. Evaluate actual final damage and equipment eligibility, not just raw point totals. If switching between fallback earrings would require another AP reset, model that cost too.
Thus DEX and LUK scrolls can be pooled by tier when all DEX is convertible; otherwise track them separately. Use the cheapest accessible equivalent tier. Do not value STR as LUK. Crit Damage and Crit Rate affect expected combat damage, not uncritical displayed range. INT/evasion do not directly increase physical attack range.
